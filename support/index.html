Support

Something not working, or a question? We read everything.

Contact us Open a request at github.com/Callan-Watkins/wildlens/issues. For anything private, such as a data deletion request, just say so and leave out personal details; we'll follow up.

Common questions

Why can it only identify plants?

Without the on-device identifier, WildLens uses Pl@ntNet, a plant specialist. Download the identifier from the Home screen or Settings (about 97 MB, one time) to identify animals, insects, birds and fungi too.

"You've used today's free cloud IDs"

Cloud identification is limited to 5 a day per phone so it stays free for everyone. The on-device identifier is unlimited and works offline. Cloud IDs reset each day.

How accurate is it?

On recent iNaturalist photos it never saw, the on-device identifier picks the exact species about 3 times in 4, and the right one is in its top 5 about 9 times in 10. When it says "Confident" it's usually right. When it's unsure, it shows look-alikes to choose from. Never eat or handle anything based on an app.

What does "Belongs here?" mean?

Range Check compares the species with checklists and records for where you are: native, introduced, invasive (only when an official register says so) or rarely recorded. It needs your approximate location; turn it on in Settings → Location.

The download stopped or failed

Check you have about 200 MB free and a good connection (Wi-Fi is best), then tap Try again. The file is checked before it's installed, so a broken download is never used.

"Ask about it" isn't showing

It needs Apple Intelligence: a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings, and the model finished downloading.

How do I stop sharing, or delete what I shared?

Settings → You. Turn off Help improve WildLens to stop sharing, or tap Delete my shared data to erase everything you shared. Your collection on your iPhone stays. See the Privacy Policy.

The app asks me to update

An older version is no longer supported. Update WildLens from the App Store; your collection is kept.

Plants, animals, insects and fungi spotted by WildLens explorers. Every photo is free to download and use, with credit.

    How photos get here.

    Only from people who turn on “Show in the public gallery” in the app. Their location is never shown.

    1

    Checked on the phone

    Photos with a face or a person in them stay private and never leave as gallery photos.

    2

    Checked again

    An automatic check makes sure each photo is a living thing, with no people, text or anything unsuitable for kids. Anything unclear stays hidden.

    3

    Reported? Removed.

    Anyone can report a photo. It leaves the gallery straight away while it's looked at. People can delete their photos anytime.
